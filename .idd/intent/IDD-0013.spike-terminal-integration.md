# IDD-0013.spike-terminal-integration

A spike is active research only while the question is unresolved. When resolved, move durable product behavior into a spec, move durable architecture decisions into an ADR, and delete the spike unless it remains useful as active research.

## Question

Which terminal surface and validated duplex exec integration can satisfy IDD-0008 within the selected Uno desktop stack, with acceptable licensing and operational behavior?

## Constraints

Preserve interactive TTY input/output, ANSI/VT, UTF-8, selection/copy/paste, resize, Ctrl+C semantics, and cancellation. Respect ENG-0001 and ENG-0003 without assuming ordinary HttpClient responses provide an interactive stdin channel.

## Method

Compare viable terminal surfaces and their licenses against the required interactions. Obtain real Engine evidence for named-pipe duplex exec creation/start, terminal exchange, resize, command failure, EOF, and session closure. Separate platform/protocol feasibility from visual rendering feasibility.

## Result

Unresolved. The source identifies terminal surface availability/licensing and hijacked exec integration as major technical risks, but does not establish a validated component or completed integration result.

## Recommendation

Resolve these uncertainties before accepting terminal implementation conformance. Record the selected durable choice in its owning Engineering Rule or ADR only after the choice is established; keep IDD-0008 as the product behavior contract.
