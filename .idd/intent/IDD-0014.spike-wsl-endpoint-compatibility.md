# IDD-0014.spike-wsl-endpoint-compatibility

A spike is active research only while the question is unresolved. When resolved, move durable product behavior into a spec, move durable architecture decisions into an ADR, and delete the spike unless it remains useful as active research.

## Question

What validated Windows-accessible endpoints are available across the targeted Docker/WSL configurations, and what would a separate secure bridge require for an Engine exposed only through a WSL Unix socket?

## Constraints

The MVP contract is IDD-0002. A WSL-only Unix socket is not promised as a direct Windows connection. Do not enable insecure TCP, require routine elevation, install/start the runtime, or silently expand the MVP to include a bridge. TCP/TLS scope remains a separate unresolved product decision.

## Method

Assess a configuration matrix including Docker Desktop with WSL2, other Docker-compatible named-pipe runtimes, and standalone WSL Engines with only a Unix socket or an explicitly configured secure Windows-accessible endpoint. Verify actual connectivity and permissions rather than inferring reachability from context names or filesystem paths. Evaluate a bridge only as separately scoped future compatibility research.

## Result

Unresolved. The source identifies standalone WSL socket access as an open compatibility risk and proposes a future bridge, but supplies no validated bridge result or accepted bridge implementation choice.

## Recommendation

Keep unsupported-endpoint diagnostics explicit and document verified configurations. A newly required bridge or additional transport needs an explicit scope decision and durable compatibility contract before becoming implementation work.
