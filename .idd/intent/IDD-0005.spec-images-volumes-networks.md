# IDD-0005.spec-images-volumes-networks

## Intent

Inspect images, volumes, and networks and delete resources with visible dependency and data-loss consequences.

## Related Specifications

IDD-0001, IDD-0003, IDD-0004, IDD-0006, IDD-0010.

## Behavior

### Images

List/search images and show tags/names, ID, creation time, and size. Inspect exposes available OS, architecture, configuration, layers, and raw JSON. Delete requires confirmation and defaults to non-force deletion. An image used by a container produces visible dependency/conflict information rather than an automatic forced request.

### Volumes

List volumes and expose name, driver, scope, mountpoint, and labels. Inspect and Delete are available. Before deletion, retrieve and show all found related containers, including stopped containers. Warn that volume deletion can permanently remove data. A daemon dependency conflict is explained and refreshed; the UI must not encourage endless blind retries.

### Networks

List networks and expose name, driver, scope, internal status, and available attachment information. Network details show IPAM and attached containers/endpoints from the inspected network rather than assuming the list response is complete. Before deletion, show attached-container dependencies and reject/block deletion by default when dependencies remain. Disable Delete for the built-in bridge, host, and none networks.

## Product-Significant Architecture And Constraints

Relationships are obtained from authoritative Docker resource/filter/inspect contracts, not a persisted dependency database. Dependency prechecks improve confirmation but cannot remove a race between the check and daemon deletion; handle the daemon's final response.

### Unresolved image scope

Image Pull and Push are explicitly unresolved in the source. Neither is a required imported capability. Pull is suggested for late MVP or a later release; Push and registry credentials are suggested for later work. Their inclusion, authentication UX, and progress handling require a product decision.

## Non-goals

- Browsing or modifying container/volume filesystem content.
- Automatically removing related containers to complete resource deletion.
- Registry login or credentials management as an implied consequence of image listing.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Each resource can be listed, inspected, and deleted through an explicit confirmation subject to daemon permissions and dependencies.
- Volume confirmation includes both running and stopped related containers and a data-loss warning.
- Network details show inspected attachment/IPAM information, and built-in networks cannot be deleted through the UI.
- Dependency conflicts leave a useful explanation and current resource state.

## Verification

Evidence covers images referenced by containers, volumes referenced by stopped/running containers, built-in and attached networks, missing resources, dependency races, and daemon conflicts. Confirm canceled operations cause no mutation and ordinary delete never silently uses force.
