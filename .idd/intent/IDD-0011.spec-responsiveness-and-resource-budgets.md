# IDD-0011.spec-responsiveness-and-resource-budgets

## Intent

Keep the application lightweight and usable during unavailable/slow Engine I/O, synchronization, and sustained live streams.

## Related Specifications

IDD-0002, IDD-0003, IDD-0006, IDD-0007, IDD-0008, IDD-0009.

## Behavior

- Docker I/O, waiting, parsing, and stream delivery do not block UI interaction.
- Requests and live sessions support cancellation and release resources when no longer needed.
- Resource tables and logs are virtualized.
- Log, stats, event-buffering, and other live-stream retention remain bounded.
- Continuous streams are opened only for relevant active/user-initiated views, with bounded concurrency rather than unrestricted per-resource work.
- A disconnected or stopped Engine leaves the shell responsive and provides a recovery state.

## Product-Significant Architecture And Constraints

The source defines performance targets for a stabilized developer workstation: average idle CPU below 1%, approximate idle working set no more than 250 MiB, and an initial local snapshot within two seconds for about 100 containers/resources, excluding a slow daemon. These are measurement targets, not unconditional guarantees on every host.

Logs and stats have separate bounded allocations. UI work is delivered in responsive batches rather than one dispatch per arriving fragment/line. Long-running streams are governed by explicit cancellation rather than ordinary short-request lifetimes.

### Unconfirmed measurement and tuning details

The benchmark hardware, exact workload, sampling window, daemon-latency boundary, and interpretation of approximate targets require a defined measurement baseline. Proposed concurrency counts, request timeouts, backoff delays, batching intervals, and log-buffer capacities remain profiling candidates.

## Non-goals

- Unbounded streams, history, or in-flight operations.
- Mandatory exact tuning numbers copied from examples/recommendations.
- Promising a snapshot latency independent of daemon performance.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Slow, stalled, canceled, or disconnected Docker operations do not freeze navigation or recovery UI.
- Sustained output beyond rendering speed retains bounded memory.
- Closing active views releases their sessions.
- The agreed benchmark workload measures the stated idle/snapshot targets, with daemon delay accounted for.

## Verification

Evidence covers slow responses, never-ending streams, cancellation, daemon restarts, log bursts, long-lived Stats, and large virtualized collections. Performance acceptance requires recorded workload/host conditions; source recommendations alone do not establish conformance.
